## Target and features

y = -ln F_H. Features: n_2q, n_1q, n_qubits, depth_2q, support_size, log2_support = log2(support_size), n_2q_offsupport = n_2q (1 - support_size / 2^n_qubits). The last term is the interaction between gate count and the fraction of the output space outside the ideal support: a fault moves output mass off the ideal distribution, and the Hellinger overlap only loses the part of that mass that lands outside the support, so the per-gate cost in -ln F_H grows with (1 - support_size / 2^n).
