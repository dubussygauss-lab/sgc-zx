# Cost model refit on Selene depolarising data

Generated by `python model/build_selene_cost_inputs.py`, then `python model/cost_model.py --family-a results/cost_model_selene_input_A.csv --family-b results/cost_model_selene_input_B1.csv --prefix selene_`, then `python model/report_selene_cost.py`, all from the repository root. The second command writes `results/cost_model_selene_metrics.csv`, `results/cost_model_selene_coefficients.csv`, `results/cost_model_selene_fig.{pdf,png}` and `results/cost_model_selene_autoreport.md` directly. Proxy numbers are read from `results/cost_model_metrics.csv` (unchanged).

## Data

- [F] Selene rows: A 432 (n = 4, 6, 8, 10, pipelines P0, P1, P2), B1 36 (n = 5, 7, 9, 10, from 12 circuits). Proxy rows: A 540 (n = 4, 6, 8, pipelines P0ref, P0, P1, P2, P3), B1 35 (7 circuits).
- [F] Features n_2q, n_1q, support_size from the Selene CSV. n_qubits and depth_2q joined from `family_A_counts.csv` / `family_B1_counts.csv` on (file, pipeline). n_2q and n_1q agree with the counts files on every row. support_size agrees with the proxy CSVs on all overlapping rows.
- [F] Target F_H = F_H_selene_depol / F_H_selene_noiseless_sameseed. On the 345 rows with a proxy row, mean (target - F_H_proxy_exact) = +0.00039, mean |.| = 0.00220. Raw F_H_selene_depol: -0.00514 / 0.00583. Dividing by F_H_ideal_sampled_mean instead: +0.00043 / 0.00224. Rows with corrected F_H > 1 (y < 0): 52.
- [O] The same-seed ratio is used because the proxy target is the exact Hellinger fidelity and the ratio removes the finite-shot bias of the raw sampled F_H to within the sampling noise. The two correction conventions are equivalent to 4e-5 in mean |.|.
