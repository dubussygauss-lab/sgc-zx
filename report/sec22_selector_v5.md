`[F]` PSEL is the pass selector in `pipelines/select.py`. It is a choice between pipelines and adds no compilation stage of its own. It compiles the input circuit with P0 and with P2 through `pipelines/compile.py` unchanged. It drops a candidate that raises an error, leaves the native gate set or fails the exactness check of `pipelines/compile.py:check`. Of the survivors it returns the compiled circuit with the lowest HQC at the requested shot count. An HQC tie goes to the lower n_2q and a further tie goes to P0. Above 20 qubits no exactness check is run and the candidate is marked ok-unchecked. The family totals of PSEL in Section 1.2 are computed in `value/selector_ledger.py` from the existing P0 and P2 rows of `value/value_ledger.csv` and `results/family_B2_counts.csv`. They are not a rerun of `pipelines/select.py`.
