[F] The official pytket-quantinuum compilation at optimisation level 3 (L3) is a stronger baseline than level 2 (L2) on both families. Its total HQC is 13.71 % below L2 on B1 and 23.16 % below L2 on family C. [F] PSEL beats L2 but loses to L3 in total, with saved -9.92 % on B1 and -8.37 % on family C. [F] Among the five compilers L2, L3, ZXG, QK3 and PSEL, PSEL alone gives the lowest HQC on 3 of 14 B1 circuits and 6 of 20 family C circuits. [F] ZXGraphlikeOptimisation followed by L2 (ZXG) and Qiskit optimization level 3 rebased to H2 natives (QK3) both cost more than L2 in total on both families. [F] Applying teleport_reduce before L3 and keeping the cheaper of the two (PSEL3) saves 3.49 % against L3 on B1 and 2.34 % against L3 on family C. [O] The saving claimed against L2 does not survive against L3, so any HQC claim in the submission should be stated against L3 and should rest on PSEL3, not on PSEL.

Notes. hwb8 is left out of the B1 totals because its ZXG and P2L3 runs timed out at 180 s. Family C circuits are above 12 qubits, so they are count-only and not checked for exactness. All B1 outputs pass the unitary or statevector check.
