# Conservative PSEL4: lowest HQC among verified candidates

Generated by `python3 value/psel4_conservative.py` from `value/psel4_ledger.csv` and the PyZX miter verdicts in `results/family_C_equivalence*.csv`. No compilation. HQC at 1000 shots. Per-circuit rows: `value/psel4_conservative.csv`.

`[F]` Rule. A candidate (P0, P2, L3, P2L3) counts as verified if (a) its ledger status is a passed full unitary check (up to 8 qubits) or a passed state check (9 to 20 qubits), or (b) a PyZX miter verdict "proven" exists for that circuit and pipeline. The variant keeps the verified candidate with the lowest HQC (ties as PSEL4: lower n_2q, then P0, L3, P2, P2L3). With no verified candidate it keeps P0, the pytket-quantinuum default output, and flags the circuit "default, unverified".

`[O]` Rule (a) accepts numerical exactness checks, so this variant is not a proof-only selection.
