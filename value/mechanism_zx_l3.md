## Conclusion (written by hand from the tables above, not regenerated by `report`)

- [F] GreedyPauliSimp is kept (lowers n_2q) in L3 on 180 of 180 A, 13 of 14 B1 and 9 of 15 C circuits.
- [F] Q1, B1: teleport_reduce cuts the input T-count from 1161 to 669 (42.38 %). After L3 the 14 circuits carry 746 non-Clifford gates, after P2 656 and after P2L3 701. L3 has more non-Clifford gates than P2 on 11 of 14 circuits and fewer on none.
- [F] Q1, C: teleport_reduce cuts the T-count from 3254 to 2076 (36.20 %). L3 leaves 2540 non-Clifford gates, P2 2930 and P2L3 2535. L3 is below P2 on 12 of 15 circuits.
- [F] Q1, A: teleport_reduce changes no non-Clifford phase (7560 before and after, 0.00 %). L3 leaves 8720 non-Clifford gates and P2 9016.
- [F] Q3: where L3 beats P2, the HQC gap is 234.2 on 11 B1 circuits (196.0 of it from 98 fewer two-qubit gates), 554.8 on 8 C circuits (486.0 from n_2q) and 1227.0 on 176 A circuits (608.0 from n_2q, 619.0 from n_1q).
- [F] Q2: where P2L3 beats L3, the HQC gap is 67.4 on 4 B1 circuits (60.0 from n_2q), 23.6 on 3 C circuits (20.0 from n_2q) and 2.4 on 12 A circuits (all from n_1q).
- [F] Q4: across 29 B1 and C circuits the ZX-only gain falls with T-density (Spearman rho -0.44, p 0.016, family C alone rho -0.64, p 0.010, B1 alone rho -0.24, p 0.412). Circuits at or above the median T-density 0.384 gain 0.20 %, those below it 2.72 %. Family A gains 0.00 %.
- [O] Verdict: the hypothesis partly holds. It holds for the two-qubit count, which is where L3 beats P2 on every family. It fails for phase merging on B1, where L3 leaves more non-Clifford gates than teleport_reduce and P2L3 still removes 45 of them. Teleportation and GreedyPauliSimp therefore merge different phases. The small ZX-only gain on top of L3 comes from L3 recovering most of the HQC through two-qubit resynthesis, and the residual ZX gain sits in low T-density circuits.
- [O] Caveat: a non-Clifford gate count is not a T-count. One PhasedX or ZZPhase after squashing can carry the product of several rotations, so the L3 and P2 columns are gate counts in the H2 set, comparable with each other but not with the PyZX T-count.

- [F] pytket-quantinuum 0.59.3 `QuantinuumBackend.default_compilation_pass`, level 3 branch (installed `quantinuum.py` lines 622 to 668): AutoRebase to a Clifford+rotation set, then `GreedyPauliSimp(allow_zzphase=True, only_reduce=True, thread_timeout=timeout, trials=10)`, then the level 2 decomposition, rebase and squash without FullPeepholeOptimise.
- [F] GreedyPauliSimp docstring (pytket 2.18.4 and docs.quantinuum.com/tket/api-docs/passes.html): it "converts a circuit into a graph of Pauli gadgets to account for commutation and phase folding, and resynthesises them using a greedy algorithm adapted from arxiv.org/abs/2103.08602". only_reduce: "Only returns modified circuit if it has fewer two-qubit gates."
- [F] Kissinger and van de Wetering, "Reducing T-count with the ZX-calculus", arXiv:1903.10477: phase teleportation "allows non-Clifford phases to combine and cancel by propagating non-locally through a generic quantum circuit" and "does not change the number or location of non-phase gates".
